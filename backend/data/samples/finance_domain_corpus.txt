The company's total revenue for the fiscal year increased by 12 percent, driven primarily by growth in its cloud services segment and higher average selling prices across its hardware portfolio.
Gross margin expanded to 41.3 percent from 38.7 percent in the prior year, reflecting a favorable product mix and continued efficiency in the supply chain.
Operating expenses rose in absolute terms but declined as a percentage of revenue, as the company realized leverage from prior investments in sales and marketing.
Management noted that foreign currency fluctuations reduced reported revenue by approximately two percentage points during the period.
The board of directors declared a quarterly cash dividend of 0.24 dollars per share, payable to shareholders of record as of the close of business on the stated date.
Free cash flow for the year was 3.1 billion dollars, an increase of 18 percent, supporting both the dividend and an expanded share repurchase authorization.
The company's balance sheet remained strong, with cash and marketable securities of 9.4 billion dollars and total debt of 4.2 billion dollars.
Deferred revenue grew 22 percent year over year, an indicator of continued momentum in subscription and recurring revenue streams.
The effective tax rate for the period was 16.5 percent, lower than the prior year due to the resolution of certain uncertain tax positions.
Research and development spending increased to 14 percent of revenue as the company accelerated investment in artificial intelligence capabilities.
Inventory levels normalized during the second half of the year following elevated balances driven by earlier supply constraints.
The company issued forward guidance projecting mid-single-digit revenue growth for the coming fiscal year, with continued margin expansion.
Segment reporting showed that the services division contributed 34 percent of total revenue, up from 29 percent in the prior year.
Capital expenditures were directed largely toward data center capacity and manufacturing automation, totaling 2.7 billion dollars for the year.
The company recorded a one-time restructuring charge related to the consolidation of certain facilities and a reduction in workforce.
Diluted earnings per share were 6.42 dollars, compared with 5.11 dollars in the prior fiscal year, an increase of 26 percent.
Return on invested capital improved to 28 percent, reflecting disciplined capital allocation and strong underlying profitability.
The audit committee reviewed the financial statements and, together with the independent auditors, discussed the quality of the accounting principles applied.
Liquidity remained ample, with an undrawn revolving credit facility of 5.0 billion dollars providing additional financial flexibility.
Management believes the company is well positioned to navigate macroeconomic uncertainty given its diversified revenue base and resilient margin profile.
